# Hypothesis assessment — exploratory evidence

| Research claim | Evidence in final workflow | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Daily calibration lowers overall RMSE at all stations | Four improved historically; Colombo retained raw; Puttalam worsened | Not universal |
| Pentad calibration improves RMSE more consistently | Five selected corrected methods produced positive historical differences | Supported descriptively |
| Monthly calibration has broad advantages | Raw v3 retained for five stations; Galle correction worsened historically | Not supported broadly |
| Atmospheric/terrain physics always improves | Puttalam moisture selection deteriorated in historical evaluation; varied other comparisons | Not established |
| Overall RMSE improvements imply better wet-day/extreme skill | Five daily-corrected stations showed lower CSI/higher heavy-day RMSE | Contradicted in these cases |

These are *descriptive conclusions*, not formal inferential test decisions. The 1995–2000 historical evaluation was previously explored; do not claim pristine independent validation.
